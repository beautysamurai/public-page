For Digital Asset Treasuries, the paper characterizes an optimal issuance policy in a dynamic corporate\-finance framework that endogenizes market valuation, capital\-market financing, and crypto\-asset accumulation, using Strategy as a case study\.

**Practical application:** The framework may help analyze RWA product design, issuance terms, liquidity, and disclosure in secondary markets\. However, the abstract does not establish direct applications to electronic execution, order design, market making, or interest\-rate products\.

**Methodology:** The paper classifies RWA tokenization into standard RWA tokenization, stablecoins, and Digital Asset Treasuries\. It then develops a security\-design model with asymmetric information and a dynamic corporate\-finance model, characterizes the optimal issuance policy, and illustrates the latter with Strategy\.

**Limitations:** The abstract does not provide the models' detailed assumptions, equilibrium characterization, empirical validation, estimation results, or performance comparisons\. External validity for actual RWA markets, transaction costs, liquidity, regulation, and credit risk is therefore unclear\. Direct implications for electronic trading and rates markets also appear limited\.

**Why read it:** The paper has a connection to market microstructure through continuous secondary\-market trading and adverse selection, but its primary focus is the economic theory of RWA tokenization, security design, and corporate finance\. It is outside the core focus on electronic execution, interest\-rate models, and yield curves, so it is not recommended for priority coverage\.
