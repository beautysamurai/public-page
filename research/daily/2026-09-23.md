**Summary:** The paper develops a stochastic\-control model integrating the make\-take strategies of a single market maker trading multiple European calls with an exchange’s rebate design\. **Practical application:** An exchange could estimate the minimum marginal rebate required to induce a one\-tick improvement in the best quote, conditional on option\-specific spread targets and the market maker’s inventory state\.
