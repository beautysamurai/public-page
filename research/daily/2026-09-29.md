**Summary:** The paper defines “information latency” as the elapsed time between a decision and the latest state observation\. It argues that even exact observations and rational expectations leave conditional uncertainty because the state continues to evolve\. For credit, insurance, and financial\-market applications, it theoretically analyzes how information age and update regularity affect risk compensation and competitive pricing\.

**Main result:** The paper shows that for short delays, conditional variance increases approximately linearly at a rate given by instantaneous variance, while under an OU process it converges to a finite upper bound\.
