**Summary:** This theoretical study analyzes competing market makers quoting to a trader who knows the asset value and always trades\. **Main result:** The paper proves the existence of a pure\-strategy Nash equilibrium with zero expected profit on each side for continuous, nondegenerate, bounded asset\-value distributions\. **Practical application:** The model provides a theoretical benchmark for assessing competitive quote formation\.
