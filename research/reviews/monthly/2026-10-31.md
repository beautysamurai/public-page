Without ground\-truth labels, validation reportedly uses out\-of\-sample link prediction, independent non\-trading links, and comparison with naive clustering; permutation tests and covariate adjustment compare timing, prices, and cash flows\. The author describes out\-of\-sample follower evaluation and a final\-period hold\-out for exploratorily developed team rules\.

**Limitations:** Only the abstract and Introduction were available; the full text, proofs, experimental details, and appendices were not checked\. All reported findings are author claims and have not been independently verified\. A single platform and an 88\.3\-hour window, together with absent ground\-truth labels, limit generalizability and confidence in inferred groups\. Observable coordination does not establish common ownership or unlawful manipulation, nor are causal price effects established\. The author also acknowledges that the team analysis is exploratory and that signals can be late and concentrated in one team\.

**Why read it:** Although the market setting is narrow, the research design is useful for market microstructure analysis because it separates coordination detection from executable profit opportunities and examines information leakage and profit\-accounting errors\. Recommended as a methodological reference, subject to checking the full paper\.
